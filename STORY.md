# NEON HEIST — Sunset City

*"Everybody comes to Sunset City to disappear. Some of us just never leave."*

**Setting:** Sunset City, a sun-bleached island off the Florida coast. The year is 1999.
**Genre:** open-world driving / third-person action
**Tone:** neon noir, dry humor, chrome, palm trees, bad decisions.

---

## The cast

**MARA VELEZ (29) — player character**
A getaway driver with ice in her veins. Five years ago she took the fall for a
robbery she didn't plan and kept her mouth shut. She just got out. She wants
two things: her brother, and a one-way ticket off this island. The city has
other plans.

**DEX VELEZ (24) — her brother**
Charming, fast-talking, permanently in over his head. He borrowed $40,000 from
the wrong man and has been paying it back a little at a time — with information.
He is not as innocent as Mara wants him to be.

**CAL ROURKE (54) — the antagonist**
Owner of the Neon Palms Casino. Ex-cop, current kingmaker. Cal owns half the
port, a third of the police force, and every favor that matters. He keeps one
book that could burn it all down: **the Sunset Ledger**.

**NADIA KING (31) — the fixer**
Cal's right hand. She moves money, cars and bodies without leaving fingerprints.
She has been looking for a way out of Cal's shadow for years. She sees in Mara
the same exit she's been too afraid to take.

**OFFICER HALE (46) — the crooked cop**
Cal's man in blue. Shows up when things go wrong, always with the same line:
"That's a shame."

**THE LEDGER**
Every bribe, every body, every payoff since 1989. It lives in Cal's office safe
on the casino's top floor. Nadia wants it. Mara needs it. Cal would kill for it.

## ACT I — HOMECOMING

### 1. Last Call
Mara arrives at Sunset Pier on the morning ferry, five years sober of this
place. Dex is waiting in a borrowed green sedan, grinning like nothing
happened. He drives her to the Sunset Motel and promises things are different
now. They are not.

By nightfall Cal's men have turned the motel room over. Dex confesses: he owes
Cal forty grand, and he's been feeding him names to stay alive.

**Gameplay:** walk the pier, meet Dex, enter his car, drive to the motel.
Tutorial for movement, camera and vehicles.

### 2. Repo Man
Cal offers Mara a deal: one job wipes Dex's debt. A pearl-white sports car is
sitting at the marina, owed to Cal by a man who stopped paying attention.
Mara takes it. The owner calls the cops.

**Gameplay:** steal the marked sports car, survive a two-star chase, deliver it
to the Neon Palms garage.

---

## ACT II — THE SCORE

### 3. Debt Collection
Cal sends Mara to collect from three businesses that are late: a pawn shop, a
tattoo parlor, and a club on the strip. The last debtor runs. Cal's point gets
made with a car chase and a pair of handcuffs in the rear-view.

**Gameplay:** drive between three collection points, chase down a fleeing
debtor, lose the responding cops. First real gunfight.

### 4. The Inside Man
Nadia asks Mara to meet at the pier after midnight. It's a setup — Cal's men
come out of the containers shooting. Mara and Nadia fight side by side.

Nadia tells her the truth: the Ledger exists, it's in Cal's office, and Dex
has been photographing pages for weeks. Dex isn't just a screw-up. He's been
trying to buy their way out.

**Gameplay:** survive three waves of goons at the pier. Protect Nadia.

### 5. Ocean Drive
To open Cal's office safe, Nadia needs the six-digit code. The code is in the
head of the only man who resets it: Cal. The only time he leaves the casino is
to watch his favorite street race. So Mara enters it.

**Gameplay:** five-checkpoint street race against Nadia (and traffic). Win to
earn Cal's attention and the safe code.

---

## ACT III — BURN IT DOWN

### 6. The Heist
Mara walks into the Neon Palms as a guest. She plants three charges, cracks
the safe, and walks out with the Ledger. Then the alarms go off.

Cal's voice on the casino PA: *"Mara. You should have stayed on the ferry."*

**Gameplay:** plant 3 charges in the casino, steal the Ledger, escape with a
three-star wanted level and lose the cops at the marina.

### 7. Sunset
Cal takes Dex to the pier. He wants the Ledger back, and he wants Mara to watch.
Mara arrives with a borrowed gun and no plan.

Cal runs for his car. Mara chases him down the pier and ends it the way this
city ends everything: metal, fire, and water.

**Gameplay:** pier shootout, destroy Cal's car, final dialogue at sunset.

**Epilogue:** Mara burns the Ledger on the beach. Dex asks what happens now.
*"We get out,"* she says. The camera pulls back over the water. The city
glows. Somewhere a radio plays.

**THE END — SUNSET CITY WILL REMEMBER.**

## Mission structure in-game

Seven story missions, chained. Each mission opens with a letterboxed dialogue
briefing and has objective markers, a reward, and a short outro card. After
mission 7, free roam continues with random delivery jobs and the full city
unlocked.

**Rewards:** $500 → $800 → $1,200 → $1,500 → $2,000 → $3,000 → $5,000.

**Wanted level:** 0–5 stars. Crimes raise it; time and distance lower it. Cops
spawn on foot and in cruisers. At 3+ stars the chase gets serious.
